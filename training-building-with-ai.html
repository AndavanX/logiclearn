Building with AI

A hands-on introduction to building small AI-powered projects — chatbots, simple automations, and creative tools.

Join the Next Cohort

This is the track for students who want to move from using AI tools to building with them. Starting from zero, we walk through creating small, real projects — a simple chatbot, a basic automation that connects a couple of tools together, a creative project using an AI API — using modern no-code and low-code platforms that don't require a computer science background to get started.

The goal isn't to produce production-grade software; it's to demystify what's actually involved in building an AI-powered tool, so the idea stops being abstract. Most students are surprised by how approachable this is once someone walks them through the first project.

For students who want to go further, this track naturally feeds into more serious technical study — but it's genuinely useful on its own for anyone who just wants to say they've built something real with AI rather than only consumed it.

  • Projects: A simple chatbot, a basic automation, a creative AI tool — built, not just discussed.
  • No prerequisites: Starts from zero, no prior coding experience assumed.
  • Tools: Modern no-code / low-code platforms, chosen to lower the barrier to entry.
  • Outcome: A real, working project you built yourself.
