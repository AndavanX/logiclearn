Scheduling & Reminders

Automatic booking, confirmations, and no-show reminders — no more back-and-forth texting to lock in a time.

Book a Free Automation Audit

Booking an appointment by text or phone usually takes five or six messages back and forth before a time actually sticks. We set up automated scheduling that shows real availability, confirms the booking the moment it's made, and sends a reminder before the appointment — all without anyone on your team typing a word.

No-shows are the expensive part. A simple reminder sent at the right time (not too early, not a surprise an hour before) cuts no-show rates meaningfully for most service businesses. We tune the timing and wording to your type of business — a haircut reminder and a consulting-call reminder shouldn't read the same.

This connects to the calendar you already use — Google Calendar, Calendly, Acuity, or whatever your booking system is — so you're not maintaining a second calendar or learning new software.

  • Booking: Customers see real availability and book without a phone tag.
  • Confirmations: Sent immediately, by text or email — their choice.
  • Reminders: Timed to actually reduce no-shows, not just to exist.
  • Rescheduling: Self-serve link so a change doesn't need a phone call either.
