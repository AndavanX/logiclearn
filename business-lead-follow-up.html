Lead Follow-Up

Timely follow-ups to new inquiries, so a lead doesn't go cold just because you were busy running the business.

Book a Free Automation Audit

The single biggest reason small businesses lose winnable leads isn't price or fit — it's speed. A quote request or contact form submission that sits for two days has already lost most of its heat. We set up automated first-response follow-ups that go out within minutes of an inquiry, in your voice, acknowledging the request and setting the next step.

This isn't a generic autoresponder. The AI reads what the person actually asked for and tailors the reply — pulling in relevant pricing, availability, or next steps based on their specific inquiry, then flagging it for you to take over the real conversation.

For leads that go quiet after the first contact, we also set up a short, non-pushy follow-up sequence (2-3 touches over a couple weeks) so inquiries don't just get forgotten in an inbox — a common, fixable source of lost revenue for busy owners.

  • Speed: First response within minutes, not whenever someone gets to the inbox.
  • Personalization: Tailored to what the person actually asked, not a form letter.
  • Follow-through: A short, polite sequence for leads that go quiet.
  • Handoff: You take over as soon as it's a real conversation, not a form reply.
