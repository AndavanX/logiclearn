Inventory & Order Tracking

Flagging low stock and summarizing order trends, so re-ordering stops being a guessing game.

Book a Free Automation Audit

Running out of a fast-moving item — or over-ordering a slow one — usually comes down to not having a clear, current picture of what's actually selling. We set up simple automated tracking that watches your stock levels against your sales pace and flags what needs attention before it becomes a problem, instead of you noticing a shelf is empty.

On the reporting side, we build short, plain-English summaries of order trends — what's moving, what's not, what's trending up — pulled from your existing point-of-sale or inventory system, so you're making re-order decisions from an actual pattern instead of a hunch.

This is deliberately lightweight: we're not selling you a new inventory platform. It connects to what you already use and adds the alerting and summarizing layer on top.

  • Low-stock alerts: Based on your actual sales pace, not a fixed arbitrary number.
  • Trend summaries: Plain-English, not a dashboard you have to learn to read.
  • No new system: Works with your existing POS or inventory tool.
  • Best for: Shops and small retailers juggling re-ordering by memory.
