Invoicing & Data Entry

Pulling data out of receipts, forms, and emails into your spreadsheets or accounting software — automatically.

Book a Free Automation Audit

A lot of small-business admin time goes into retyping information that already exists somewhere: a receipt into an expense sheet, a paper form into a database, an emailed PO into your accounting software. We set up AI tools that read those documents and extract the relevant fields directly into the system you already use.

This works well for recurring, structured-ish documents — vendor invoices, expense receipts, intake forms, order confirmations — where the AI learns the pattern of what you need pulled out and where it goes. It's not meant to replace a bookkeeper's judgment; it's meant to remove the retyping so their time goes to the parts that actually need a person.

We build in a review step before anything posts to your books, so mistakes get caught before they become a reconciliation headache later.

  • Sources: Receipts, invoices, intake forms, and emailed documents.
  • Destinations: Your spreadsheet, QuickBooks, Xero, or whatever you already run on.
  • Review step: Extracted data is checked before it posts, not auto-committed blind.
  • Time saved: Usually the biggest win for solo founders and small back offices.
