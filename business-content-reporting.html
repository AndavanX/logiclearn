Content & Reporting

Drafting social posts, weekly summaries, and reports from your existing data — in minutes instead of hours.

Book a Free Automation Audit

Keeping up a consistent social presence or writing a weekly business summary is easy to let slide when you're busy running the actual business. We set up AI drafting tools that turn your raw material — recent sales, a photo, a quick note about what happened this week — into a first-draft post or report in your voice, ready for you to review and send.

For internal reporting, this usually means pulling numbers you already have (sales, bookings, inquiries) into a short weekly or monthly summary that's actually readable, instead of a raw export nobody opens.

Everything is a draft, not an auto-publish. You stay the one deciding what goes out — the tool just removes the blank-page problem of starting from nothing.

  • Social posts: Drafted from your own updates, sales, or photos.
  • Reports: Weekly or monthly summaries in plain English, not a raw spreadsheet.
  • You approve everything: Nothing auto-publishes without your review.
  • Time saved: Mostly the blank-page time, not the thinking time.
