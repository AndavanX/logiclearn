Romance & Chatbot Scams

AI chatbots that sustain long, personalized conversations to build trust before asking for money.

See All Red Flags

Romance scams used to require a real person investing real time in a long con. AI chatbots now let a single scammer run dozens of convincing, emotionally attentive conversations simultaneously, remembering personal details and responding with the kind of warmth and consistency that makes the relationship feel genuine over weeks or months.

The ask, when it finally comes, is usually framed as a one-time emergency — a medical bill, a stuck shipment needing a customs fee, travel money to finally meet in person — and by that point, the emotional investment makes it hard to apply the skepticism the situation deserves.

A consistent pattern across these scams: the relationship stays entirely digital (calls get avoided or are suspiciously brief and vague), the story has an emergency with a financial solution, and meeting in person never quite happens despite repeated promises.

  • The giveaway: Any request for money, no matter how it's framed, from someone you've never met in person.
  • Red flag: Avoids video calls, or calls feel oddly generic and brief.
  • Red flag: A recurring pattern of near-misses on meeting in person.
  • What to do: Reverse-image-search their photos, and talk to someone outside the relationship before sending anything.
